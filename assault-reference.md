# Assault Reference

CC-6221/54 “Scorch”

RC-5130/76 “Noble”

### Usage

Assault covers the basic tenets of breach and entry methods, as well as the tools and equipment utilised to

best succeed in a frontal or direct action.

### Equipment

Assault forces utilise a selection of tools and equipment to succeed.

- DP-23 Shotgun

The DP-23 Shotgun is a short range weapon used to quickly clear small spaces. It comes with the ability to

fire slugs (for longer range engagements) or buckshot (for close quarters). Additionally, Bean Bag rounds

can be loaded into it. Once they hit their target, it's the same as getting Zeus cuffed.

- Personal Shield

The personal shield is equipped as a grenade, and activates a front facing gunshield when activated to

protect its user from limited firepower in the direction which the weapon is facing. Only works on Primary

Weapons.

- Riot Shield

The Riot shield is one of the most effective means of protecting oneself from Small arms fire. By clicking

Ctrl T with either the DC-15S or the DP-23 shotgun, you can equip it. It stops anything short of Explosives or

High Caliber rounds.

- Lockpicks

Basic instruments used to proceed past locked doors. Faster lockpicks are more likely to break, but can

disable locks quicker when successful.

- DB-90 Breaching Charge

A small explosive used to blow open doors and entryways (not including FOB doors/gates).

- Sonic Detonator

A non-lethal grenade device that functions like a flashbang. While it is unlikely to kill or seriously injure, it

can still cause harm to the subject(s).

### Entryways

A doorway is a breacher’s worst enemy. Doorways, entryways, and other such points of ingress or egress

are often referred to as “fatal funnels”. It is the objective of the breacher or assaulter to move through and

away from this fatal funnel as rapidly and efficiently as possible in any situation.

### Markers

When a room, building, or space has been cleared successfully, use blue spray paint to mark a highly visible

location on the building (such as next to the entryway) to indicate that it is clear. Note that hostiles may

re-enter or re-populate a once-cleared location if given the opportunity to do so.

### High Value Targets

Oftentimes, breachers must take into consideration the presence of a high value target or object that

cannot befall harm. In such circumstances, the breacher must employ a non-lethal method to disable or

incapacitate the subject. Stuns are effective against biological enemies, whilst ions are effective against

droids.
