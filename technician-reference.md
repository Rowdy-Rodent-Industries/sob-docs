# Technician Reference

### TECHNICIAN REFERENCE

Alpha-104 “Spider”

## Usage

Technicians or Techs for short, rely on their vast arsenal of Hacks, Drones, and Equipment to deal untold levels of damage to enemies. They excel in Both Offensive and Defensive Tactics when used to their fullest.

## Equipment

### Prowler 500 (UAV)

The Prowler-500 Recon Drone is a device used by recon & tech specialists to obtain an aerial view of a location from a distance to discover information more safely.

The drone must first be set up, then connected to a UAV terminal for use. The drone may be deployed and later repacked when not in use.

When in manual control, the Prowler-500 Recon Drone follows your chosen rotary control scheme.

The drone may also be automatically assigned. [Shift] + [Left Mouse Click] brings up autonomous movement controls, and [Control] + [Right Mouse Click] can be used to give multiple orders. [Right Mouse Click] on the arrow to define the different types of control.

Type: Drone movement orders

Behaviour: How the drone behaves

Altitude: What height the drone will move to

− Mouse Droid (MSE-6) MSE-6 droids can be used for surveillance or for delivering a payload to an enemy target. To effectively use an MSE, the user will need all of the following pieces of equipment.

| Droid Caller | The Droid caller is the piece of equipment required to activate and deactivate the MSE. |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance Kit | The Maintenance kit is a piece of equipment that allows the user to remove the fuse and change the selected fuses. |
| MSE-6 Series Manual | The Operations Manual will appear next to the fuse panel to assist the user with their selections. |

The MSE-6 has 4 Payload types, you can select from the following.

| Safe | The MSE will not explode. |
| --- | --- |
| Random | The MSE will randomly select between EMP or EXPLOSIVE. |
| EMP | The MSE will release an EMP explosion. |
| Explosive | The MSE will release a normal explosion. |

After the Payload type you have the Operation types.

| Surveillance | The MSE will be used for recon, using only its driving functionalities. |
| --- | --- |
| Courier | The MSE will be used to deliver small objects, the user will be able to insert small items into the MSE |
| Demining | The MSE will be used to deliver an explosive payload, the user will be able to detonate the MSE on command |

Following the Payload type you have the data protection mode. Basically you can choose between destruction on hit or destruction on immobilization.

| On hit | If the MSE takes any amount of damage, the MSE will detonate. |
| --- | --- |
| On Immobilization | If the MSE becomes immobilized, the MSE will detonate. |

Lastly you have the Side relations, before anything else make sure you have connected the Mouse Droid to your UAV terminal. Changing the MSEs Side to anything other than Blufor before connecting it to the terminal will lose your access to the droid.

| RETAINER | The MSE will be on the side of the user. |
| --- | --- |
| OPFOR | The MSE will be on the OPFOR side. |
| BLUFOR | The MSE will be on the BLUFOR side. |
| INDEP | The MSE will be on the INDEP side. |

### Blaster Turret

The Blaster Turret is a placeable UAV that shoots at Opfor forces either independently or Manually. The Tech can connect to it and shoot Targets by using the UAV terminal to connect to it. Similar to connecting to a Prowler 500.

### Auto Turret

Auto Turrets are a throwable grenade that once it lands deploys a small defense turret. It is connectable and requires no setup besides the grenade throw action. However, just like the Blaster Turret, it will automatically shoot at Opfor.
