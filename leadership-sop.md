# Leadership SOP

## Overview
This document describes all powers, responsibilities, and limits of each level of leadership. This document is not an end all guide for what can and cannot be done, but an outline for the leadership core to follow. 

All leadership within SOB, from CLC to CC are expected to enforce and follow the rules at all times. They are the members of the unit charged with keeping order and ensuring that all members of our community are able to enjoy the experience we provide. All conversations within leadership channels are private and may not be “leaked” or shared with anyone outside of leadership unless given permission to do so by UC. 

Also keep in mind that we are a power-by-billet unit rather than a power-by-rank, just because you are a CLC doesn’t mean you have the power to change or do anything within the community. Responsibilities are given to those who earn it, and by billet alone. 

Suppose at any point Command staff feels that a member of the leadership team isn’t doing their job, creating problems, or not meeting the expectations laid out in this document. In that case, we can and will re-evaluate your rank and position within the leadership core. 

## Brief/Debrief SOP
Generally Brief and Debrief should be a time for only leadership to discuss plans, ideas, or issues about the operation. This is time for no one outside of the leadership structure to interrupt or cause issues as its not prevalent to them. Below is the structure for those briefs.

### In game Briefing
In game briefings should only consist of Billeted Brigade, Company, Platoon, and Detachments for that day. Billeted squad leads may be there on company's digression. Any reservists, including reserve squad leads, should be outside of the brief and wait in the staging area or around the fob/ship for company and platoon to organize them. 

### Out of game Debriefing
Out of game debriefings should only consist of Billeted Brigade, Company, Platoon, Detachment, and Squadleads for that day. Reservists squad leads may be there on company's digression. Any other reservists/members should bring up in their individual briefs but should not be there for the actual leadership debrief. This is a place to bring up issues and comments from that current operation. This is not a place to fight or disrespect others, as these conversations need to be productive to be functional.

## Promotion Information

Ideally, in this unit, we do not hold people back from ranks for arbitrary reasons, however, leadership ranks will almost always require some level of competence to be promoted to the next rank. For Squad leads, they should be going into the leadership corps as soon as they’re eligible; however, if they are not proving competent, the platoon/company lead can hold back their promo. 

People may skip ranks if they meet requirements; there is no requirement to promote them through the ranks, However, any rank that requires “Good performance” needs time in the rank prior to being promoted to said rank. So, someone who is a squad lead should be promoted to CP before CS, regardless of the training and specs to prove competency. 

1IC and 2ICs cannot be the same rank. The 2IC will need to wait until the 1IC gets promoted before getting the next available rank. 

When entering leadership ranks that require all of the specs, your maximum rank is what will require all specs, and the ranks prior will require supplemental specs. For example, if you are a CC, you will start as a 2LT until you get 2 specs, to which you can be promoted to 1LT, and then three specs to Captain. The maximum rank for your individual billet will require the full specs. 

## Leadership Structure

### Junior NCOs

Junior NCOs are the lowest level of NCOs and are typically brand new to the leadership team. They are simply there to learn how to be a leader and to lead their squad, however, they have no actual power or position to write DAs, or handle disciplinary issues within the squad, platoon, or company. 

They will however have access to the NCO chat so they may see all information being passed along throughout leadership, as well as access to the DA roster to see when and who are receiving DAs. 

### NCOs

NCOs will make up the bulk of the leadership team. Similar to JNCOs they have no real ability to handle DAs or disciplinary problems.  Both NCOS and JNCOs have the ability to put people up for awards but may not vote on them.

### COs

COs are at the forefront of the unit. These members have the ability to give DAs, vote on Awards, change things within their respective elements (with UC approval), and have relative power within their element. These members are the ones entrusted with running the day-to-day needs of the unit. 

### Squad Leads/2IC

Squad leads and 2ICs of a squad are in charge of just that, their squad. They make sure that the 2 other members with them are doing as they are supposed to, showing up to ops, and completing training as needed to prepare for the Specialization exam of their choosing. They must go to their respective Company Staff for billeting and unbilleting purposes however they have the power to choose who is and isn’t in their squad. No one else can force members into these squads, Squads must unanimously agree to the member before billeting. 

Squad leads and 2ICs are also responsible for the well-being of their squad. They directly interact with them daily so if something is going awry, or a member is struggling the squad leads are responsible for working with their squad and their COC on it. 

P2 squad leads are capped at CP, and Specialized squad leads are capped at CS. P2 Squad 2ICs are capped at CLC and Specialized 2ICs are capped at CP. 

### Platoon Leads/XO

Platoon leads and XOs are in charge of their respective Platoons. They have no power over billeting as that falls to the CO or Company Lead, and the majority of their job is ensuring that their squads are working to specializations, people are showing up to ops, and that their platoon is performing to the level they are supposed to. 

Platoon leads are capped at Second Lieutenant and XOs are capped at CSM, both require both specializations to reach. 

Platoon Lead/XO is typically a P2 billet however they have the ability to run a Specialisation like everyone else, below are the ways they can. They must be certified in both specializations to run them. 
If a platoon Lead or XO is billeted within a squad in their platoon, they must run the specialization of that squad, with that squad. They cannot be a lone commando.
If neither the Platoon Lead or XO are billeted then they will run as a command squad, where they can declare a specialization to run as in their billeted ops, and be eligible for awards given to the respective billeted specialization. 
If you declare a specialization and wish to change you must discuss it with your CO beforehand, and wait for approval before switching over. 

This has no effect on the prefix to your name, as you are in a P2 billet and therefore Identified by Rank. 

### Company Leads/XO

Company Leads and XOs are the COs of the unit and have the powers described above. They are directly in charge of the company under them and only have the power to change things within their company. They have the ability to billet and unbillet as need be, run tryouts for platoon leads and XOs, and work with their advisor to prepare P2 squads for their exam. 

Company Commanders are capped at Captain and XOs are capped at First Lieutenant, both required both specializations to acquire. 

Company Leads and XOs also have the ability to be proctors for specialization Exams, these will be chosen by the UC alone and trained to proctor the exam to the expectations of the UC. Once a certified proctor, they have the ability to schedule and run exams as needed for all companies. After running an exam and if the exam takers fail, they have the power to either reschedule or inform their respective platoon lead/advisor that they are not ready for the exam and must be readvised before scheduling the next exam. 

Company Leads and XOs are the only members of the unit allowed to proctor exams and as such comes a heavy responsibility. This is not to be abused however their position gives them a unique perspective on the unit, and the ability to give feedback, advice, and FTXs as they see fit.

### Detachment Leads/XO

Detachment Leads and XOs have the power to billet, unbillet, and change their detachment or training as they see fit (with approval from UC). They are the people charged with running a detachment and have the ability to do so as they see fit. They have the responsibility to ensure that the detachment is performing to the quality and expectation set by the UC as well as that the needs of the unit are met by that detachment. They should be actively working with other COs as well as the mod team to ensure that they’re performing to the best of their ability. 

Detachment COs are capped at First Lieutenant, and XOs are capped at Second Lieutenant, in this instance only, specializations are not required.

Detachment CO/XO billets are active billets, meaning that the CO and XO hold active billets outside of the sections. Detachment COs and XOs no longer count to the restrictions for in op players. For example if there are 4 billeted pilots in op and the CO shows up, then they can fly as a 5th pilot. 

# Order 4

If at any point all members of brigade become indisposed for any period of time longer than 7 days, it is the responsibility of the Company Commanders and detachment leaders to form what will be known as the Command council, whose sole purpose is to keep the day to day of the unit running. They will have the power to change, add or remove things as necessary so long they have a unanimous vote. They will have no power to change key components of the unit, discord, teamspeak, arma server, or any other SOB product other than what they would normally have the power to do. Should this council remain in power longer than 2 months, they will have the power to elect a new interim Unit Commander from those sitting on the council,  which will maintain power until the original members of brigade return.
