# Demolition Reference

### DEMOLITION REFERENCE

CC-6221/54 “Scorch” RC-5130/76 “Noble”

## Usage

The Demolitionist is an essential part of any team, trained and assigned specifically for scenarios of sabotage or dynamic entry. They use powerful explosives to disable or destroy enemy assets from near or far, or to create points of access where previously stood a wall.

## Equipment

The Demolitionist carries with them several key pieces of equipment for their work.

### Advanced Firing Device

The Advanced Firing Device, or AFD, is used to connect with placed explosives and trigger a detonation. It has an approximate range of 5 kilometres, and allows for the use of advanced triggers, such as by timer, proxy sensor, infrared sensor, or tripwire.

### Marker Flags

Marker flags are used to identify safe routes of passages or areas with known explosives. Blue marker flags should be placed alongside a route of safe passage through an area with explosive hazards on either side of the path to create a corridor, in order to indicate that the line of travel is clear. Red marker flags should be used to mark known dangers and explosives.

### Defusal Kit

The defusal kit is an essential piece of equipment used to dismantle or disable explosives. It must be administered to the explosive directly, for which extreme caution is advised.

### Nacht-10 Yellow Smoke Grenade

Yellow smoke is used to visually mark a demolition target to ensure that those in proximity are both visually and verbally warned of the danger.

## Explosive Ordnance

The bread and butter of a Demolitionist’s kit is the explosive material available to them.

Below is a list of the most commonly used explosives:

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Det Pack | Standard explosive. |
| MED | Similar to the Det Pack in firepower, but can be either thrown or launched out of a grenade launcher or UGL. Magnetises on contact to a surface before detonation. |
| Throwable Charge | A large and heavy satchel charge. Doubles the Det Pack and MED in firepower. |
| EC-01 | Similar to the MED, but more effective against armoured targets. |
| DP-3 | A Commando-specific explosive which packs enough firepower to destroy an MTT. |
| APERS Mine Dispenser | A gadget which, when placed, may scatter small anti-personnel mines in an area in front of it. |
| M4A1 Slam | A directional explosive effective at mine-clearing and injuring and killing light infantry. Has a kill-zone of 100m. |
| M18A1 Claymore | A larger, more powerful variant of the M4A1 Slam. Has a kill-zone of 100m in a 45° angle in front of it.. |
| VS-50 APERS Mine | A standard anti-infantry mine. |
| HX Anti Tank Mine | A pressure plate mine highly effective against tanks. |
| APERS Tripwire Mine (PMR3) | A standard anti-infantry mine, but attached to a string used to trigger a detonation when tension is applied. |
