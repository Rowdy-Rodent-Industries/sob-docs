### Usage

Advanced Insertions teaches unit members how to effectively gain entry to an otherwise defended or

contested area.

### Equipment

- JT-12 Clone Jumppack

The JT-12 Jumppack is a backpack item with the ability to deliver jumps in two modes (long and short).

Long jumps provide both an altitude increase and horizontal distance, whereas short jumps are directional

only.

- Hawkbat Parachute

The Hawkbat Parachute is only functional in freefall, and should be opened roughly 200m above the DZ or

ground.

### Insertion Types

Various situations may demand specific methods of insertion to ensure trooper survival and efficient entry.

- High Altitude, Low Opening

HALO inserts are to originate about 2km directly above the target DZ. A single short jump is necessary right before landing to negate fall damage.

- High Altitude, High Opening

HAHO inserts involve a drop origin that is much further from a drop zone, often to avoid exposing the aircraft to anti-air emplacements which may fortify the objective. The required altitude of the drop point is about 2x the horizontal distance which must be covered to the DZ (for example, a DZ 3km away horizontally would need to have a drop point at least 6km in altitude).

Utilise the long jump as much as possible to cover the horizontal distance required, and avoid frequent adjustments with [A] or [D].

- Hot Drop

Hot Drops are done at Impulse speed, at an altitude of below 100m. An immediate short jump will be required in order to reduce momentum and negate fall damage.

- Rappelling

Rappelling, or fast-roping, is done roughly 40m above the ground, and requires that the aircraft is stationary above the DZ.

### Jump Master

The Jump Master must be a CO/SL for the purposes of HALO, HAHO, or Rappel insertions. The Jump

Master for a Hot Drop is always the Pilot.
