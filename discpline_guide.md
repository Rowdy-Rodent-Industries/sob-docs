# Discipline

## Unit Rights

1. All SOB members have the right to know what they're being disciplined for and who wrote the report

2. All SOB members have the right to be respected and be treated fairly during the DA process.

3. All SOB members have the right to have full transparency on their DA or warning

4. All SOB members have the right to see all evidence presented before them prior to a full write-up

5. All SOB members have the right to protest any DA given there is probable cause on any basis in the rules presented in this document.

    - a. Probable cause means that either at a point during the DA process, one of their rights was infringed upon, or a rule in another part of this document was broken.

    - b. You have the right to a single protest per DA

6. All SOB members have the right to request a CO outside of the DA to oversee the DA

    - a. Brigade staff cannot be overruled

7. All SOB members have the right to confidentiality, and no DA or warning will be spread to members outside of the leadership staff without their permission.

8. All SOB members cannot be disciplined/punished more than once for a single instance of a rule infraction.

9. All SOB members who are banned have the right to a single appeal. If the appeal is denied, they may not come back to the unit permanently.

## Unit Discipline

1. Any member of the unit will be given a warning for their actions unless there is substantial evidence that warrants punishment.

2. All punishments as a result of a DA must be within the reason of the DA

3. All DAs must have proper evidence for the punishment provided

4. All banned members have one additional chance to appeal to Brigade staff if they would like to rejoin the community

5. All rule-breakers are subject to punishment at the handling of a COs discretion

6. Lying, Cheating, or anything of this nature when involved with any punishment will automatically get you banned from the unit, no questions asked.

7. Unfit punishment may be revoked by Brigade Staff at any time.

8. All punishments must be filled with proper information and if this expectation is not met then the punishment may be rescinded

9. All NCOs and Officers are required to keep leadership channels private, leaking of any kind will be punished.

10. Any DA written must be open to the entire leadership staff. Defendants cannot and will not be able to participate in the investigation, discussion, decision, or final verdict.

11. All members are subject to being removed from the unit for breaking a rule at any point.

12. There are two types of disciplinary actions within the unit, Leadership DAs would be anything that pertains to this document, SOPs or any other general rule breaks. Staff DAs pertain to brigade staff departments, so things like zeus team, recruitment team, etc etc
