### Usage

Advanced Medical training covers the application of medical equipment, substances, and treatment

methods to effectively keep your team members battle-ready. This does not include the very basic items

such as bandages, tourniquets, and the like taught in Basic Training.

### Equipment

Medical personnel utilise a selection of tools and equipment to succeed.

- RFK/X Medisensor

The RFK/X Medisensor is a device used to establish patient vitals, including pulse, blood pressure, and

blood volume. The device is to be used on the patient’s chest, and takes 10 seconds to use.

- Field Defibrillation Unit

The FDU is a secondary item slot equipment that can be used to immediately reestablish a patient’s heart

rate. Causes significant pain. This item is carried by Commandos and Paramedics.

- Bacta

Bacta is known widely for its restorative properties. Bacta Patches are ideal for treating a severely damaged

limb, but are bulky and require more time to apply. Bacta Sprays are excellent at treating minor wounds, and

are quickly applied.

- Stretcher

A stretcher is a piece of equipment carried uniquely by Field Support Paramedics and can prevent patients

from bleeding out whilst they are on it (though they can still perish via other means).

### Medications

Medics are capable of administering a variety of substances to treat the wounded or improve the efficacy

of their team. As with blood transfusions, medications must be administered into an extremity that does

not have a tourniquet applied.

- Batroxobine

Batroxobine is a coagulant, used to slow blood loss.

- Deraformine

Deraformine is a drug used to induce a death-like state, effectively knocking the patient unconscious. It takes roughly 10 seconds for results to show.

- Latheniol

Latheniol is a fast-acting euthanasia solution which is heavily restricted across the galaxy. After 30 seconds from the time of administration, the patient will be paralysed. After 60 seconds from the time of administration, the patient will die. The use of Latheniol must be approved by a Field Support paramedic or by a CO, and only as a last resort.

- Nevastrin-8

Nevastrin-8 is a stimulant used to increase a patient’s heart rate, and is lethal when combined with morphine. Consult medisensor before administering.

- Pentabenzadrenolyne

Pentabenzadrenolyne is a fast-acting stimulant known colloquially as PBA, or “commando crack”. A single dosage of PBA will induce increased pain tolerance, movement speed, and heart rate. A second dosage of PBA will induce a state of “overdrive”, which includes high aggression and blood clotting (so the patient cannot bleed out), but results ultimately in a heart attack and death.

- Vutalamine

Vutalamine is a depressant which moderately reduces heart rate and movement speed, but also greatly reduces weapon sway.

### Medical Radio

Assigned medics are required to be present on SR425 so that their aid may be requested and made use of

when nearby to the location of a mass casualty incident.

### Blood Content

A total blood content of 3000mL or lower is often fatal to a patient. Blood should be transfused to keep the

patient’s blood levels above this threshold.

An unconscious patient must be of above blood content 5100mL and present with a heart rate in order to

be reoriented.
