# SOB Constitution

Written by CC-1452/62 “Rat”

## Summary

The purpose of this document will be to set the rules and guidelines of SOB. This will be an all-encompassing document of all rules within SOB. Any violations of the rules will result in disciplinary action. This document is also a living document and has the capacity to be changed, updated, or removed at any time if need be.

## Rules

### General Rules

1. All members of the community must comply with all rules written within this document and any other public document while representing SOB

2. All aspects of trolling, spamming, or harm to anybody within our TeamSpeak, Discord, or official servers are prohibited

3. Racism, Disrespect, Harassment, or anything with ill intent is prohibited in any way shape or form.

4. The use of hacking, glitching, exploiting, or using any client-side mods not strictly stated within the server information channel is not allowed.

5. Administrative abuse in any way is prohibited at all times.

6. All members must comply with and obey the written rank structure

7. All members are to respect the RP, and the immersion of an operation at all times

8. All members are to adhere to the Kit guide at all times

    - a. Company Staff within operations do not count to the limit of weapons within a platoon b. Additional facewear and faces not provided in the arsenal is not permitted at any time i.
        - IE, maul faces, bandana facewear, or any other additions that aren’t directly added to the arsenal ii.
        - The only faces allowed are clone M/F, or custom faces earned through awards.

9. Kits cannot be mixed and matched. Equipment from one spec/detachment cannot be used on other spec/detachment kits.

    - a. IE, if you have a trooper custom, you cannot wear the trooper custom when running ARC

    - b. The Only exception to this is with Alpha gear, as it can be worn within ARC, Trooper, and JT kits

10. Dual uniting is allowed within reason, you may not be within another unit that interferes with your billeted operation duties.

11. Intentional Blue on Blue is not tolerated at any time for any reason

12. Lone Wolfing of any kind is prohibited at any time.

    - a. This includes acts that could serve to lone wolf, like triple timing without orders, jumping ahead of a platoon, or purposely moving away from your squad.

13. Long Range frequencies must remain professional at all times

14. Do not deviate from your designated role’s equipment for any reason

15. The Chain of Command must be followed at all times

16. While a part of transport vehicles, respect the crew and leadership within that vehicle.

    - a. Communications should be kept strictly to information on an as-needed basis.

17. Field Requisitioning enemy equipment and vehicles is only allowed with SL+ approval when absolutely necessary to ensure survival a. When field-requisitioning vehicles, this must be communicated to all members within an operation b. Field-requisitioned equipment should only be used until you have the ability to re-kit, or use a SOB vehicle/weapon

18. You must run as your billeted role on your billeted day, you may not change your specialization mid-op.

    - a. When running as a reserve, you may not change your spec mid-operation.

    - b. If you are a spec squad, attempting to bring in a member to their squad, you may run as a P2 squad to trial the member.

18. Baiting other members into breaking the rules is prohibited at any point.

    - a. Recording other members with ill intention counts as baiting.

19. All community members are allowed to drink or use illicit substances on SOB servers, however, failure to act responsibly will result in temporary removal from the servers.

    - a. Your actions while under the influence of any kind will be held against you as if you were sober.

20. All streams representing SOB operations must follow all unit rules

21. All streams representing SOB operations must follow all twitch TOS

22. Private conversations of any kind cannot be streamed

23. If streaming in operations, use the streaming tag on TeamSpeak

24. You cannot stream trainings or Exercises

25. No NSFW content is allowed on any SOB servers, this includes but is not limited to discord,teamspeak, and arma servers

26. While switching specializations is allowed, there is a 2-month cooldown before you can switch specs.

27. While in operation, Melee combat should be kept to an as-needed basis and only used if no other alternatives are available.

    - a. If there is an attempt at melee while friendlies are actively engaging a target, any damage done to the player will not be counted as blue on blue.

28. All limited weapons per kit guide, are also limited to one per person

    - a. Cannot carry two limited weapons at the same time

### Gamemaster Rules

1. Targeting specific players at any point is prohibited

2. Zeus controlling with the intent to kill is not allowed

    - a. You may control to disable players and vehicles but not to kill

3. Zeus’s should always aim to provide the player with the best possible experience

4. Zeus Should never direct players in their plan of an operation, they may suggest or hint at certain aspects but it is the role of the company and platoon to plan out operations

5. The difficulties and scale of an operation, objective, or task should make sense per the operation.

    - a. Operations should have logical reinforcements, dynamically implemented b. Scaling operations and tasks based off of the element tasked with it is acceptable within reason

6. Excessive usage of artillery is not allowed.

7. IDF of any kind should have ranging rounds, you shouldn’t directly strike an element with the first round without warning.

8. Objectives should be set up dynamically so that it doesn’t affect a player's experience.

    - a. AA should not be set up mid-operation without warning

9. Captured players should always give consent to being captured for an extended period of time.

    - a. If they would prefer to be killed and reinserted that is their right b. Capturing of any kind must be dynamic, you cannot capture a trooper and TP them across the map within seconds Captured players should always be captured with either class 1 or 2 restraints

10. Any captured HVTs that are returned to base unharmed count as captured for the campaign

11. Missions must have attainable objectives

    - a. Operations intended to cause failures are allowed, within reason

12. Missions must be followed close to lore

    - a. No senseless slaughter of civilians.

13. Missions must have OPORDS provided at least 2 days in advance

    - a. Limitations must be provided on an OPORD, with brigade approval.

    - b. No OPORD means no restrictions can be imposed.

14. Main operations must have mission files submitted at least 2 days before the operation

15. PVP operations must have brigade approval

    - a. This is for both Main Operations and Skirmishes

16. Actors must have brigade approval

    - a. Lead zeus is responsible for any actions done by actors in an operation

17. All zeus’s must be open to constructive criticism, as feedback will be provided for all operations

18. Zeus training is required to host an operation.

    Teamspeak/Discord Rules

19. All SOB members are subjected to all rules on any platform including both Teamspeak and discord

20. All SOB members are to abide by Discord and Teamspeak TOS

21. All names on any official SOB server must be accurately displayed as to what is on the orbat

22. Explicit images, links, or gifs are strictly prohibited

23. Political, controversial, and possibly offensive conversations must be kept within reason.

    a. If conversations turn malicious or ill-intended then they must stop or they will be met with swift action

24. All posts within the discord are subjected to being deleted without notification

25. All discord and teamspeak means of communication and chats are to be used for their intended purpose.

### Guest Rules

1. All guest must abide by all rules provided in this document

2. Any guest may not use any equipment that they would otherwise not be authorized to use within their home unit.

    - a. If a guest is not apart of any unit, then they must use the SOB Recruit equipment provided on the Kit guide

3. If a guest’s aux mod is not included in the SOB mod pack, the guest can create an equivalent kit with existing equipment provided in the mod pack.

4. You may not impersonate any member of a unit, or represent a unit you are not a part of.

5. Zeus must approve the use of specialized equipment, such as vehicles or emplacements, prior to the operation stepping off.

    - a. Specialized includes Detachment and non-standard equipment

6. All SRG members may be invited to skirmishes and main operations, with CO approval

a. If SRG members show up, they must either fall in line with a SOB p2 squad, or SOB equivalent squad b. If SRG members have enough to form their own squad, they may do so and fall under a platoon

7. All former members of the unit will be given unit friend tags, and are free to play on our servers for any fun op. Any official operations such as Main ops or Joint ops, must be approved by Brigade staff

### Leadership Rules

1. NCOs and Officers must abide by all rules present and are required to enforce the rules to the highest degree

2. All NCOs and Officers are required to work with, train, and help improve all aspects of the unit and the general appearance of the unit to others

3. NCOs and Officers must oversee their own element first, and if an NCO or Officer has a problem with a trooper out of their authority they are allowed to deal with it but must report the incident to that trooper’s NCOs and Officers

4. As an NCO or Officer, you are held to a higher standard and must be reachable and active in some way. If you are determined to be inactive or unreachable, an investigation will be conducted into your absence.

5. All leadership roles within SOB will happen on a trial basis, with all members given the ability to trial.

    - a. Brigade at any point reserves the right to reject or deny certain members the ability to trial for positions based off of past history, DA’s, or requirements for the position

### SOB Strike System

1. As a member of the Special Operations Brigade, you are obligated to reply to attendance posts each week.

2. Unbilleted members must respond to, at minimum, 1 attendance post with either present or absent each week

3. Billeted members must respond to, at minimum, their billeted operation day with either present or absent each week

4. If you fail to meet the above minimum requirements you will receive a strike. If you receive 3 strikes you will be removed from the unit for lack of engagement.

5. A strike is removed when you respond to an attendance post, for example, if you fail to respond one week you will receive a strike, if you respond to the next post, your strike is removed.

6. If you receive a strike you will be notified via DM.

7. If you must go on an ELOA (LOA for longer than 1 month), you will be moved out of your squad and into reserves. You may keep all of your quals, specs, and awards upon return.

8. If you are absent from 3 or more billeted operations consecutively, regardless of sign-ups, you are subject to removal from your billet by your immediate CO

### Reserves

1. Members who have obtained an Order medal may request to be placed in reserves

2. Members in Reserves do not have a requirement to attend any operations and are exempt from the strike system, but periodically may be required to respond to a check-in to remain in the unit

### Skirmish Rules

1. You must only use equipment that you're permitted too on standard operations.

2. To run an Operation you must first become an approved Zeus.

3. To become an approved Zeus, you need to be cleared by a Zeus Instructor.

4. In order to run an operation you must reserve the Operation in the correct channel.

5. All members visiting other unit's Skirmishes/Fun ops must run Phase 2 equipment as stated in the kit guide.

6. All specialized designations are permitted to use their standard equipment only if they have a team/squad of 3-4 of the same type (3-4 RC for example) and the host Mission Maker/Zeus approves it.

7. The only exception to this is detachment personnel with flight training, they may only fly on other unit's servers if given permission by the host Mission Maker/Zeus, otherwise they should use

    - a. Phase 2 equipment (unless they are qualified in other roles then the above two rules apply)
