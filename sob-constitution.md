# SOB Constitution

Written by CC-1452/62 “Rat”

## Summary

The purpose of this document will be to set the rules and guidelines of SOB. This will be an all-encompassing document of all rules within SOB. Any violations of the rules will result in disciplinary action. This document is also a living document and has the capacity to be changed, updated, or removed at any time if need be.

## Rules

General Rules

1. All members of the community must comply with all rules written within this document and any

other public document while representing SOB

1. All aspects of trolling, spamming, or harm to anybody within our TeamSpeak, Discord, or official

servers are prohibited

1. Racism, Disrespect, Harassment, or anything with ill intent is prohibited in any way shape or form.

1. The use of hacking, glitching, exploiting, or using any client-side mods not strictly stated within the

server information channel is not allowed.

1. Administrative abuse in any way is prohibited at all times.

1. All members must comply with and obey the written rank structure

1. All members are to respect the RP, and the immersion of an operation at all times

1. All members are to adhere to the Kit guide at all times

a. Company Staff within operations do not count to the limit of weapons within a platoon b. Additional facewear and faces not provided in the arsenal is not permitted at any time i.
IE, maul faces, bandana facewear, or any other additions that aren’t directly added to the arsenal ii.
The only faces allowed are clone M/F, or custom faces earned through awards.

1. Kits cannot be mixed and matched. Equipment from one spec/detachment cannot be used on

other spec/detachment kits.

a. IE, if you have a trooper custom, you cannot wear the trooper custom when running ARC

b. The Only exception to this is with Alpha gear, as it can be worn within ARC, Trooper, and JT

kits

1. Dual uniting is allowed within reason, you may not be within another unit that interferes with your

billeted operation duties.

1. Intentional Blue on Blue is not tolerated at any time for any reason

1. Lone Wolfing of any kind is prohibited at any time.

a. This includes acts that could serve to lone wolf, like triple timing without orders, jumping ahead of a platoon, or purposely moving away from your squad.

1. Long Range frequencies must remain professional at all times

1. Do not deviate from your designated role’s equipment for any reason

1. The Chain of Command must be followed at all times

1. While a part of transport vehicles, respect the crew and leadership within that vehicle.

Communications should be kept strictly to information on an as-needed basis.

1. Field Requisitioning enemy equipment and vehicles is only allowed with SL+ approval when

absolutely necessary to ensure survival a. When field-requisitioning vehicles, this must be communicated to all members within an operation b. Field-requisitioned equipment should only be used until you have the ability to re-kit, or use a SOB vehicle/weapon

1. You must run as your billeted role on your billeted day, you may not change your specialization

mid-op.

a. When running as a reserve, you may not change your spec mid-operation.

b. If you are a spec squad, attempting to bring in a member to their squad, you may run as a

P2 squad to trial the member.

1. Baiting other members into breaking the rules is prohibited at any point.

a. Recording other members with ill intention counts as baiting.

1. All community members are allowed to drink or use illicit substances on SOB servers, however,

failure to act responsibly will result in temporary removal from the servers.

a. Your actions while under the influence of any kind will be held against you as if you were sober.

1. All streams representing SOB operations must follow all unit rules

1. All streams representing SOB operations must follow all twitch TOS

1. Private conversations of any kind cannot be streamed

1. If streaming in operations, use the streaming tag on TeamSpeak

1. You cannot stream trainings or Exercises

1. No NSFW content is allowed on any SOB servers, this includes but is not limited to discord,

teamspeak, and arma servers

1. While switching specializations is allowed, there is a 2-month cooldown before you can switch

specs.

1. While in operation, Melee combat should be kept to an as-needed basis and only used if no other

alternatives are available.

a. If there is an attempt at melee while friendlies are actively engaging a target, any damage done to the player will not be counted as blue on blue.

1. All limited weapons per kit guide, are also limited to one per person

a. Cannot carry two limited weapons at the same time

Gamemaster Rules

1. Targeting specific players at any point is prohibited

1. Zeus controlling with the intent to kill is not allowed

a. You may control to disable players and vehicles but not to kill

1. Zeus’s should always aim to provide the player with the best possible experience

1. Zeus Should never direct players in their plan of an operation, they may suggest or hint at certain

aspects but it is the role of the company and platoon to plan out operations

1. The difficulties and scale of an operation, objective, or task should make sense per the operation.

a. Operations should have logical reinforcements, dynamically implemented b. Scaling operations and tasks based off of the element tasked with it is acceptable within reason

1. Excessive usage of artillery is not allowed.

1. IDF of any kind should have ranging rounds, you shouldn’t directly strike an element with the first

round without warning.

1. Objectives should be set up dynamically so that it doesn’t affect a player's experience.

a. AA should not be set up mid-operation without warning

1. Captured players should always give consent to being captured for an extended period of time.

a. If they would prefer to be killed and reinserted that is their right b. Capturing of any kind must be dynamic, you cannot capture a trooper and TP them across the map within seconds c.
Captured players should always be captured with either class 1 or 2 restraints

1. Any captured HVTs that are returned to base unharmed count as captured for the campaign

1. Missions must have attainable objectives

a. Operations intended to cause failures are allowed, within reason

1. Missions must be followed close to lore

a. No senseless slaughter of civilians.

1. Missions must have OPORDS provided at least 2 days in advance

a. Limitations must be provided on an OPORD, with brigade approval.

b. No OPORD means no restrictions can be imposed.

1. Main operations must have mission files submitted at least 2 days before the operation

1. PVP operations must have brigade approval

a. This is for both Main Operations and Skirmishes

1. Actors must have brigade approval

a. Lead zeus is responsible for any actions done by actors in an operation

1. All zeus’s must be open to constructive criticism, as feedback will be provided for all operations

1. Zeus training is required to host an operation.

Teamspeak/Discord Rules

1. All SOB members are subjected to all rules on any platform including both Teamspeak and discord

1. All SOB members are to abide by Discord and Teamspeak TOS

1. All names on any official SOB server must be accurately displayed as to what is on the orbat

1. Explicit images, links, or gifs are strictly prohibited

1. Political, controversial, and possibly offensive conversations must be kept within reason.

a. If conversations turn malicious or ill-intended then they must stop or they will be met with swift action

1. All posts within the discord are subjected to being deleted without notification

1. All discord and teamspeak means of communication and chats are to be used for their intended

purpose.

Guest Rules

1. All guest must abide by all rules provided in this document

1. Any guest may not use any equipment that they would otherwise not be authorized to use within

their home unit.

a. If a guest is not apart of any unit, then they must use the SOB Recruit equipment provided on the Kit guide

1. If a guest’s aux mod is not included in the SOB mod pack, the guest can create an equivalent kit

with existing equipment provided in the mod pack.

1. You may not impersonate any member of a unit, or represent a unit you are not a part of.

1. Zeus must approve the use of specialized equipment, such as vehicles or emplacements, prior to

the operation stepping off.

a. Specialized includes Detachment and non-standard equipment

1. All SRG members may be invited to skirmishes and main operations, with CO approval

a. If SRG members show up, they must either fall in line with a SOB p2 squad, or SOB

equivalent squad b. If SRG members have enough to form their own squad, they may do so and fall under a platoon

1. All former members of the unit will be given unit friend tags, and are free to play on our servers for

any fun op. Any official operations such as Main ops or Joint ops, must be approved by Brigade staff

Leadership Rules

1. NCOs and Officers must abide by all rules present and are required to enforce the rules to the

highest degree

1. All NCOs and Officers are required to work with, train, and help improve all aspects of the unit and

the general appearance of the unit to others

1. NCOs and Officers must oversee their own element first, and if an NCO or Officer has a problem

with a trooper out of their authority they are allowed to deal with it but must report the incident to that trooper’s NCOs and Officers

1. As an NCO or Officer, you are held to a higher standard and must be reachable and active in some

way. If you are determined to be inactive or unreachable, an investigation will be conducted into your absence.

1. All leadership roles within SOB will happen on a trial basis, with all members given the ability to trial.

a. Brigade at any point reserves the right to reject or deny certain members the ability to trial for positions based off of past history, DA’s, or requirements for the position

SOB Strike System

1. As a member of the Special Operations Brigade, you are obligated to reply to attendance posts

each week.

1. Unbilleted members must respond to, at minimum, 1 attendance post with either present or absent

each week

1. Billeted members must respond to, at minimum, their billeted operation day with either present or

absent each week

1. If you fail to meet the above minimum requirements you will receive a strike. If you receive 3 strikes

you will be removed from the unit for lack of engagement.

1. A strike is removed when you respond to an attendance post, for example, if you fail to respond one

week you will receive a strike, if you respond to the next post, your strike is removed.

1. If you receive a strike you will be notified via DM.

1. If you must go on an ELOA (LOA for longer than 1 month), you will be moved out of your squad and

into reserves. You may keep all of your quals, specs, and awards upon return.

1. If you are absent from 3 or more billeted operations consecutively, regardless of sign-ups, you are

subject to removal from your billet by your immediate CO

Reserves

1. Members who have obtained an Order medal may request to be placed in reserves

1. Members in Reserves do not have a requirement to attend any operations and are exempt from the

strike system, but periodically may be required to respond to a check-in to remain in the unit

Skirmish Rules

1. You must only use equipment that you're permitted too on standard operations.

1. To run an Operation you must first become an approved Zeus.

1. To become an approved Zeus, you need to be cleared by a Zeus Instructor.

1. In order to run an operation you must reserve the Operation in the correct channel.

1. All members visiting other unit's Skirmishes/Fun ops must run Phase 2 equipment as stated in the

kit guide.

1. All specialized designations are permitted to use their standard equipment only if they have a

team/squad of 3-4 of the same type (3-4 RC for example) and the host Mission Maker/Zeus approves it

1. The only exception to this is detachment personnel with flight training, they may only fly on other

unit's servers if given permission by the host Mission Maker/Zeus, otherwise they should use

Phase 2 equipment (unless they are qualified in other roles then the above two rules apply)

### Discipline

Unit Rights

1. All SOB members have the right to know what they're being disciplined for and who wrote the

report

1. All SOB members have the right to be respected and be treated fairly during the DA process.

1. All SOB members have the right to have full transparency on their DA or warning

1. All SOB members have the right to see all evidence presented before them prior to a full write-up

1. All SOB members have the right to protest any DA given there is probable cause on any basis in the

rules presented in this document.

a. Probable cause means that either at a point during the DA process, one of their rights was infringed upon, or a rule in another part of this document was broken.

b. You have the right to a single protest per DA

1. All SOB members have the right to request a CO outside of the DA to oversee the DA

a. Brigade staff cannot be overruled

1. All SOB members have the right to confidentiality, and no DA or warning will be spread to members

outside of the leadership staff without their permission.

1. All SOB members cannot be disciplined/punished more than once for a single instance of a rule

infraction.

1. All SOB members who are banned have the right to a single appeal. If the appeal is denied, they

may not come back to the unit permanently.

Unit Discipline

1. Any member of the unit will be given a warning for their actions unless there is substantial evidence

that warrants punishment.

1. All punishments as a result of a DA must be within the reason of the DA

1. All DAs must have proper evidence for the punishment provided

1. All banned members have one additional chance to appeal to Brigade staff if they would like to

rejoin the community

1. All rule-breakers are subject to punishment at the handling of a COs discretion

1. Lying, Cheating, or anything of this nature when involved with any punishment will automatically

get you banned from the unit, no questions asked.

1. Unfit punishment may be revoked by Brigade Staff at any time.

1. All punishments must be filled with proper information and if this expectation is not met then the

punishment may be rescinded

1. All NCOs and Officers are required to keep leadership channels private, leaking of any kind will be

punished.

1. Any DA written must be open to the entire leadership staff. Defendants cannot and will not be able

to participate in the investigation, discussion, decision, or final verdict.

1. All members are subject to being removed from the unit for breaking a rule at any point.

1. There are two types of disciplinary actions within the unit, Leadership DAs would be anything that

pertains to this document, SOPs or any other general rule breaks. Staff DAs pertain to brigade staff departments, so things like zeus team, recruitment team, etc etc

### Brigade Staff

The Brigade staff make up the command staff and strategic vision for the unit. This group compromises the main bulk of those that run and operate large-scale and long-term changes encompassing the whole unit. The general descriptions are below

### S-Shop Commander

The S-Shop Commander is the individual charged with the management of all S-Shops. Ensuring quality control for training and administrative products in addition to the creation and delivery of unit operations.

They work directly with the Staff leads of each S-Shop to ensure smooth execution of taskings and constant unit support.

### Brigade NCOIC

The Brigade NCOIC is charged with the development and management of all leadership within the unit from the Junior NCO tier to the Commanding Officer tier. They ensure all levels of leadership perform and lead to a high standard. The Brigade NCOIC will act as 3IC for the unit assisting the Brigade CO and Brigade XO in the execution of their duties.

### Brigade CO/XO

The Brigade CO/XO are 1IC and 2IC for the unit and are in charge of the entirety of the Special Operations

Brigade. They will work with Company staff, COs, and the rest of Brigade staff to ensure day-to-day operations of the unit are running smoothly while developing the Strategic Vision for the unit going forward.

### Path to Specialization

This part of the document will entail all the information that leadership must know about exams, the process of becoming specialized, and all the bits of information that must be written down somewhere.

This part will be updated as needed; however, it should be a generalized section to cover all parts of the path to specialization.

Trooper

This is the intro role into the unit. As a trooper you have just joined and have yet to take any trainings.

To move on from the trooper phase, you must complete your basic training and at least one Specialization

Training.

Cadet Phase

The CDT Phase is the real intro phase into the unit. Once you have passed your basic training and one spec training you are considered a CDT. As you work through your other trainings to be eligible for full spec, you unlock more and more kits to play with in operation. You may run any of the spec CDT kits you have trainings for so long as your with other similar specs, and you haven’t declared a spec for your billeted day yet.

Once you have all your trainings, you can declare a spec, making you an official CDT for that spec (CDT-A/J/R) and eligible for the FTX and Passing Exercise. You must pass the FTX BEFORE you can take the PE, and there will still be a minimum month hold between the declaration of a spec and the attendance of an FTX.

CDT kits do have some limitations, to which are below

Commandos

- Limited Shields

- P2 Armor values

- Lowered Carrying Capacity

ARCs

- Cadet Vest

- No MM9

- Lowered armor and space

- Cadet Backpack

- Lowered space

JumpTroopers

- Cadet equipment

- No Kama

- One jump, jump-pack

- Lowered Carrying Capacity

Examination

The Exam in itself is a challenging feat. We offer a squad exam for each specialization, those requiring it for their chosen billet, leadership requirement, or just looking to reserve as it. Exercises require the examinees to be at their absolute best. Each exam is created to test the examinee on all training taken up to that point and the examinee's ability to perform their responsibility within their specialization. For a squad to pass, they must use every possible avenue of success.

In the event that a person fails the exam, the proctors will always give feedback, the good, the bad, and the ugly. This information is relevant information that, if honed in on, can help pass the exam in the future.

Everyone must wait at least 24 hours to take their next exam; however, it may be longer depending on timing, how the squad performed, etc. The proctors have every right to send the squad back to the Advisor

FTX and say they are unprepared for the exam.
