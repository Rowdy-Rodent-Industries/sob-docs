### Purpose and Intent

The purpose of this handout is to provide Platoon Leaders with guidance on effective planning practices and to outline their responsibilities during operational execution. The examples contained herein are intended to serve as reference tools to assist Platoon Leaders in the development of objectives for their platoon and the effective coordination and employment of their squads.

Your Company leadership will designate the load-in time for platoon leaders, most commonly once they have sent a Discord Ping for load in.

Once this has occurred your priority is to grab a kit from the arsenal and proceed directly to your Zeus/Company Pre-brief. During this time it is recommended to do the following:

1. Set your Long Range and Short Range Radios

### Company Brief

Post Zeus briefing your Company element will finalize the company level plan and Platoon Areas of Operation based off of the previously provided OPORD located in the discord(ex. friday-planning), so communicate any questions or concerns about the planning portion during this phase.

### Platoon Brief

Once the Zeus/Company Pre-Brief has concluded you will radio to your platoon to meet you in an established Platoon Briefing Area(if one is not present you will choose a location). Once you have done so, remind your platoon to update their ACE Team Management as well as their Squad Designations on the Top Right of the Map. Following this you will do the following:

1. Send your needed Reservist count to your Company CO/XO via LR 45 or via CTAB

2. Brief your Squads on the information provided during the Company Pre-brief and

your platoons Primary and Secondary objectives

3. Begin to Mark and plan your objectives in your AO(s)

4. Manage the Platoon Loadout Composition, ensure each squad's kits make sense for

the operation(ex. Not enough AT Troopers when it is expected to see Heavy OPFOR Armored presence).

5. Ensure all Medics are communicating on Medical Short Range frequency 425

6. Coordinate initial insert into your AO

### Expectations for Platoon Leadership

The Platoon Leadership element consists of both Commanding Officer(CO) and Executive Officer(XO), you are a team and all the following responsibilities fall on you to ensure success. Your CO will manage LR 45 and XO will manage LR 46. A Leadership team is only as effective as your abilities to assist each other in tasks, if one is overburdened with Long Range comms you may need to pick up their slack with directing the platoon or vice-versa. A leadership team should eventually ,with practice, grow to develop a non-verbal understanding of where they need to step in to assist the other.

1. Coordinate with Company Command over LR 45 to execute any orders they made

pass down, to inform them of any changes or intelligence in operation, and the status of your Platoon.

3. Maintain effective command and control of your Platoon, this includes frequent

6. Platoon leadership should encompass a Macro/Micromanagement of their element

to ensure a long-term and short-term tasking at all times. Knowing when to Micro vs Macro manage is important, it is the difference between giving Squad Leaders the freedom to be adaptive and creative in solving problems and having to remind people constantly to watch their spacing to prevent MASSCAS scenarios.

Macromanagement is a hands-off, strategic leadership style that focuses on high-level goals and outcomes rather than short term tasks, fostering autonomy and trust.

Micromanagement is a controlling leadership style characterized by excessive supervision, obsession with minor details, and a reluctance to delegate, which stifles autonomy, and creativity.

Map Marker Cheat Sheet

In ex.1 you will see some of the most common DRAWN MILITARY markers used during operations

1. Objective = OBJ (Objective)

2. Flag = FOB(Forward Operating Base) or HQ(Headquarters)

3. Arrow = Direction of Approach or AP (Attack Point)

4. Ambush = OW (Overwatch)

5. Destroy = FM (Fire Mission)

6. Start = Not utilized commonly but could potentially be used to show Jump Trooper

entry

7. End = DZ (Drop Zone)

8. Pick Up = LZ (Landing Zone)

9. Join = RP (Rendezvous/Rally Point)

10.Warning = Shows urgent or hostile POI 11.Unknown = POI (Point of Interest) Shows a potential point of interest 12.Dot = This can be utilized for Marking AO names (ex. AO Acklay), sectoring off objectives or to be a filler marker for planning purposes 13.Platoon Markers while not listed are another good marker to utilize for marking your Area of Operations or AOs
