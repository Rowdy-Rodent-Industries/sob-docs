### Usage

Reconnaissance is an essential component of an action or assault to an objective. Taking the steps to

identify the nature of the objective, its defences, and potential hazards or pitfalls ahead of time can be

instrumental in the success or failure of the attempt.

### Navigation

Reconnaissance specialists utilise various methods to navigate unknown or hazardous terrain effectively, using stealth to avoid being decisively engaged.

- Triangulation

Triangulation involves the use of various reference points to obtain a current position. To obtain a

retrograde bearing to the target, take your bearing and adjust it by 180°. Draw a line between your reference

point and retrograde point to get a rough idea of your location. Do the same for another reference point, and

identify your location as the point where the lines cross.

- Land Navigation

Divide the route to your objective into smaller components using waypoints to mark your progress and keep

track of your route.

### Recon Drone

The Prowler-500 Recon Drone is a device used by recon specialists to obtain an aerial view of a location from a distance to discover information more safely.

The drone must first be set up, then connected to a UAV terminal for use. The drone may be deployed and later repacked when not in use.

When in manual control, the Prowler-500 Recon Drone follows your chosen rotary control scheme.

The drone may also be automatically assigned. [Shift] + [Left Mouse Click] brings up autonomous movement controls, and [Control] + [Right Mouse Click] can be used to give multiple orders. [Right Mouse Click] on the arrow to define the different types of control.

Type: Drone movement orders
