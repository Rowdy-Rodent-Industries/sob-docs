### Usage

ARC Troopers are best used as a Supporting element to RC as they lack the shielding that makes RC great for CQC. They can be used in a plethora of ways however as they specialize in being “Jacks of all Trades”. Having access to Demo Perms by default all ARCs are capable of Defusal and Planting Explosives. The Squad Leader and Squad Medic have access to Medical perms on top of Demo Perms meaning these 2 members are also able to perform adv Medical functions. Along with the above, ARCs also have access to a couple of unique equipment that makes them well suited to just about everything.

### Weapons and Equipment

Some of the Unique equipment that ARCs have access to is the MM9 Wrist rocket system, NT-242, and Verpine Shatter Rifle. Here’s a list of the most prevalent unique assets

The Heavy RPS 6 is the main AT of ARC Troopers. It's AT Rocket wire guided and locked on meaning you can just send it at what you're looking at and guide it or lock on to AA Rocket Heavy RPS 6 something with a Thermal Signature to have it guide itself. This can only be done with the AT, the AA Rocket requires a lock on to track.

The EPL-2 is a specialized EMP launcher capable of clearing groups of non-organic enemies in quick succession. The 2-round launcher has the ability to 2rnd Magazine EPL-2 EMP Launcher cover long distances with its high velocity round before detonation at the intended target.
