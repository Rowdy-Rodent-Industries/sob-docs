# Unit Leadership Powers

## Leadership Roles
1. JNCO
    - All CLC’s and CP’s b. 
    - No power to DA, or handle disciplinary issues

2. NCO
    - Anyone from the rank of CS-CSM is an NCO
    - No power to DA, or handle disciplinary issues, Can only council members of their element.
    - Has the power to put forward awards.
    - Cannot vote on awards

3. CO
    - Anyone who is a platoon lead, company lead/XO, or detachment lead/XO is a CO
    - Has power to DA and handle disciplinary issues.
    - Should only council members of their own element.
    - Has the power to put forward awards.
    - Can vote on awards d. Change things within their element  With brigade approval.

## Billeted Roles
1. Squad Leads/2IC
    - Responsible for their squad, maintaining standard, quals, and activity 
    - Should go to their COC for adding/removing members of the squad

2. Platoon Leads/XO
    - Responsible for their platoon, maintaining standards, quals, and activity. 
    - No power over billeting however can recruit and work with company to billet.
    - Typically a p2 billet.
    - May declare a Spec.

3. Company CO/XO
    - Responsible for their company, maintaining standards, quals, and activity. 
    - Can billet and unbilled as needed.
    - Should never insert a member into a squad without squad approval.
    - Can proctor exams

## Leadership Billeting Rules

1. If a platoon Lead or XO is billeted within a squad in their platoon, they must run the specialization of that squad, with that squad. They cannot be a lone commando.

2. If neither the Platoon Lead or XO are billeted then they will run as a command squad, where they can declare a specialization to run as in their billeted ops, and be eligible for awards given to the respective billeted specialization.

3. If you declare a specialization and wish to change it, you must discuss it with your CO beforehand and wait for approval before switching.
    - a. You must also follow the cooldown in the constitution to change specs. 

## Brigade Staff

The Brigade staff make up the command staff and strategic vision for the unit. This group compromises the main bulk of those that run and operate large-scale and long-term changes encompassing the whole unit. The general descriptions are below

### S-Shop Commander

- The S-Shop Commander is the individual charged with the management of all S-Shops. Ensuring quality control for training and administrative products in addition to the creation and delivery of unit operations.
- They work directly with the Staff leads of each S-Shop to ensure smooth execution of taskings and constant unit support.

### Brigade NCOIC
- The Brigade NCOIC is charged with the development and management of all leadership within the unit from the Junior NCO tier to the Commanding Officer tier. They ensure all levels of leadership perform and lead to a high standard. 
- The Brigade NCOIC will act as 3IC for the unit assisting the Brigade CO and Brigade XO in the execution of their duties.

### Brigade CO/XO

- The Brigade CO/XO are 1IC and 2IC for the unit and are in charge of the entirety of the Special Operations Brigade. 
- They will work with Company staff, COs, and the rest of Brigade staff to ensure day-to-day operations of the unit are running smoothly while developing the Strategic Vision for the unit going forward.
